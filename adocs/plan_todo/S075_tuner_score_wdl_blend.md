## Why this exists

`tools/datagen` writes four columns and the tuner reads two of them. The third,
`score`, is the engine's own score for the position at 100000 nodes, White
relative -- and the datagen header says why it is there: "the other two are
written so a dataset can be re-filtered without replaying anything."

`adocs/eval_tuning_strategy.md` section 2.7 is the use for it:

```
target = lambda * sigma(score_from_deep_search) + (1 - lambda) * WDL_result
```

with lambda typically 0.7 to 1.0, "sometimes annealed downward during training.
Rationale: the search score is low-variance but biased toward the current
evaluator; the game result is unbiased but very high-variance. The blend beats
either alone. This is directly applicable to HCE tuning, not only to NNUE."

Today's fit is lambda 0: pure game outcome, one noisy bit spread over about 92
positions per game.

## The hazard, stated before the fit

Section 1 of the same document: "Training the eval toward its own search score at
the same depth is a fixed point that carries no information." At lambda 1 that is
exactly what this becomes, with one mitigation -- the scores in
`selfplay_v2.tsv` were produced by the engine as it was on 2026-08-14, before
S065's refit of 827 constants, so they are not the current evaluator's own
output. That mitigation expires the moment a corpus is regenerated by the engine
being fitted, and S082 and S083 both regenerate. Whoever executes this states the
generating commit next to the lambda.

## The multiple-comparison trap this step walks into

Section 7: "if you generate 20 candidate weight vectors and SPRT each at
alpha=0.05, you expect one false positive by chance." A lambda sweep is exactly
that shape. So the sweep is decided on held-out error -- which costs no machine
time worth counting, an epoch over 11.0 M rows is seconds -- and **one** vector
goes to a match. Held-out error selects; the SPRT decides. Loss improvement is
not Elo, and the audit trail should show one verdict, not five.

## Cost

Fits are minutes each. One SPRT: three to four and a half hours at the DEC-048
and DEC-050 settings.
