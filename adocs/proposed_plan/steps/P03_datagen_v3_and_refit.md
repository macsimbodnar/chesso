## Why

About 1500 parameters will exist by P26; the corpus has to over-determine
them, cover the endgame terms (P19, P24) and be quiet where the static
evaluation is asked. One position per sampled game reduces the correlation
that many positions of one game carry.

## Description it is implemented from

Grant 2020 §2.2: positions sampled from about a million self-play games at
very short controls, each replaced by the end of a high-depth principal
variation; diversity and quietness preferred over WDL precision. CPW *Texel's
Tuning Method*. Ethereal's early datasets also used other engines' positions;
that part is excluded here.

## Seeds

The node budget and sample rate are outputs of accepts (3); the candidate
grid is written in the pre-registration before any corpus is generated
(DEC-134 form 2, derivation over chesso's own data).

## Measurement

Generation is a night (DEC-155). One SPRT for the refit.
